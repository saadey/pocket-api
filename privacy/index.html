Pocket API privacy policy

Pocket API by TechieBravo. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

Pocket API is a tool for testing APIs. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't collect your data. We have no server that receives it.

Data stored on your device

Your requests, request history, collections, environments and settings are stored only on your device. Android backups of this data are turned off, so it isn't copied to cloud backup. Values you mark as secret, and saved requests or drafts with typed credentials, are encrypted using Android Keystore. Typed tokens and passwords aren't kept in history.

Requests you send

When you tap Send, the app sends that request, including any headers, body and credentials in it, directly to the server you entered. That server's own privacy policy applies, and we don't receive a copy. The built-in demo requests go to public demo services, httpbin.org, jsonplaceholder.typicode.com and postman-echo.com, only when you send them. The app doesn't connect to any server otherwise.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, Android handles your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never receives biometric data.

Files and clipboard

Export files are saved only where you choose, and they don't include secret values, typed credentials or history. Text you copy goes to your device's clipboard, where other apps may be able to read it.

Deleting your data

You can delete requests, history, collections and environments in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Pocket API is a tool for developers and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
